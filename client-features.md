# OmniBot

OmniBot is the customer desk for a business. Messages from WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram land in one place. The team replies from that desk, every person is kept on file, and an assistant answers from the business’s own website and product list.

## One inbox for every channel

**WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram together.** A customer can write on any of those channels and the conversation shows up in the same desk. The team does not have to switch apps to see who is waiting.

**A reply from the desk goes back on the same channel.** The answer leaves on the app the customer used. A private note stays inside the team and is never sent to the customer.

**Each chat has an owner.** A conversation can sit with one agent, a whole team, or stay unassigned until someone picks it up. The queue can be sorted into all conversations, mentions, chats you are part of, and chats that still need a reply.

**Labels and saved views.** Tags group chats by topic. A saved view keeps a filtered list, such as one inbox or one label, so the same search does not have to be rebuilt every morning.

**Conversation status.** A chat can be open, pending, or resolved. Resolved chats stay on the record and can be opened again when the customer writes back.

## The morning overview

**A live picture of the day.** Sign-in opens on an overview, not a blank inbox. It shows open chats, unassigned chats, pending chats, resolved chats, the contact count, and how many agents are online.

**What needs a person.** Ongoing conversations, the team’s availability, the connected channels, the week’s message volume, and recent activity sit on the same page. From there the team can open a chat, all conversations, or the contact list.

## Contacts

**Every person, from every inbox.** WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram contacts are listed together. A person who wrote on more than one channel still has one contact page.

**Where they wrote from.** Opening a contact shows the channel and the identifier that channel shares: a WhatsApp number, a Messenger ID, or an Instagram ID.

**Details the customer actually shared.** An email, a phone number, a city, or a public Instagram or Facebook link is saved when the customer types it, or when Instagram returns a username. Empty fields stay empty until that information exists.

**Add people in bulk.** Contacts can be created one by one or uploaded from a spreadsheet, so a campaign list does not have to be typed by hand.

## Campaigns

**One message, many people.** A campaign sends the same outreach to a chosen group. Live chat, SMS, WhatsApp, and Messenger and Instagram each have their own campaign list.

**Choose the audience.** A send can go to the people you select, or to everyone already on that inbox. A contact who has a WhatsApp number and a social chat can be included on the matching campaign for each channel.

**WhatsApp uses an approved template.** A WhatsApp campaign goes to phone numbers through a template the business has already had approved. Delivery can be reviewed after the send.

**Instagram and Messenger stay inside an existing chat.** Those campaigns reach people who already have a conversation on that inbox and are still inside the reply window. A typed username or a Facebook name on its own does not start a new thread.

## The assistant

**Each business has its own assistant.** The reply is written for that account. It uses the company description, a short reading of the company website, and the latest product spreadsheet.

**The website explains the business.** When a website address is saved, OmniBot reads a few public pages and keeps a short summary. Questions about what the company does are answered from that summary and the company description.

**The spreadsheet is the price list.** Stock, price, and product details come from the uploaded sheet. The assistant does not invent a price, a policy, or a stock count when the sheet and the website do not contain it. If the fact is missing, it says it will check.

**A photo has to match the exact product.** When a customer sends a picture, the assistant looks for the name printed on the item and lines it up with one row. A price is given only for that exact row. A similar color, a similar character, or a similar product gets a short question asking which item they mean.

**One reply per customer message.** The assistant answers the incoming text once. It leaves private notes and its own outgoing messages alone, so a chat does not turn into a loop.

## Running the team

**Availability.** Each agent can be available, busy, or offline, so new work can land with the people who are actually on the desk.

**Teams and assignment.** Agents can be grouped into teams. Chats can be handed to a person or a team, and assignment rules can spread new conversations across the people who should take them.

**Saved replies and macros.** Canned responses cover the lines the team types every day. A macro runs a set of steps, such as a reply plus a label, in one action.

**Automations.** Rules can label, assign, or move a conversation when something happens, so the repeat sorting does not depend on someone remembering it.

**Help center.** Articles, categories, and languages can be published for customers who want an answer before they start a chat.

**Account controls.** Inboxes, WhatsApp templates, labels, custom fields, agent permissions, roles, and an audit log of account changes live in settings. Each person sees the parts of the desk their role allows.

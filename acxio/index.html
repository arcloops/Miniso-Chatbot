Omnichannel

Every door opens onto the same desk.

A customer on WhatsApp, a customer on email, and a customer on the website are the same kind of work. acxio keeps them in one list, marked by channel, with the person named and the last line visible. The team does not keep a second app open to find out who wrote.

The reply leaves on the channel it arrived on. WhatsApp stays WhatsApp. Email stays email. The desk is the only place that changes.

Queue
Nothing waits in another tab

Mine, Unassigned, and All sit on one row. A new message joins the list as soon as it arrives, from a business number, a page, an inbox, or the website.

Agent
The first reply does not wait

Routine questions are answered in the thread. A person can take the same conversation when the question needs a name, a booking, or a decision.

History
The next person starts in the middle

Assignment, notes, and the full exchange stay with the conversation. The customer does not repeat themselves because the shift changed.

Why it is useful

A shorter queue, not another inbox.

The point of one desk is that fewer messages sit unseen, and the ones that remain already have a name, a channel, and a reply in progress.

01 Customers stay where they are

Nobody is asked to move to a different app. They write on the channel they already use, and the answer comes back there.

02 The team shares one room

Whoever is on the desk sees the same list. Ownership is visible, so two people do not answer the same thread and nobody assumes someone else has it.

03 The agent covers the quiet hours

When the room is empty, the agent still writes the first reply. The thread is warm when a person sits down, instead of a pile of unread names.

04 The screen matches the work

Inboxes on the side, conversations in the middle, the open thread beside them. The channel is a label. The work is the conversation.
